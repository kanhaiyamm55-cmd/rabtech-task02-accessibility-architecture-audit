## Prioritization guidance

- **Critical:** blocks a core task for many users or exposes a serious barrier.
- **High:** prevents or severely impairs an important task.
- **Medium:** causes meaningful friction with a workaround.
- **Low:** smaller usability issue or polish.
- Base priority on the observed impact, not just a tool score.
